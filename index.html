Our Story

Where Our Story Began

Precious Moments was born in Brooklyn, NY from a simple belief: life's biggest milestones deserve to be both created and captured with the same level of care, creativity, and heart.

What started as a passion for proposal planning and events photography has grown into a full-service creative studio. Today, we design breathtaking proposal setups, document engagements, photograph newborns, celebrate cake smash birthdays, and bring special events to life across New York City.

Every client gets a personalized experience — no cookie-cutter setups, no templates. We listen to your story, understand what makes your moment special, and design something that's uniquely yours.
